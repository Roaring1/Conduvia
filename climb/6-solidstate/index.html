    Tier 6 — Solid State¶

    Semiconductors and automation. Grow a silicon crystal, slice wafers, and let machines run the line.

    6
    Tier 6 — Solid State

    13 quests · 11 on the win-path · 1 branches

    Briefing

    Transistors, PLCs and Robots¶

    Welcome to the Information Age¶

    Tier 4 gave you a factory that runs on electrical power. Tier 5 gives you a factory that runs on logic.

    We are moving away from vacuum tubes and massive relays into solid-state electronics.

    • Silicon purification is your first hurdle. You can't print logic on dirty sand.
    • Discrete Semiconductors replace fragile glass tubes.
    • Logic Modules and Chips pack entire circuit boards into tiny packages.
    • PLCs (Programmable Logic Controllers) finally let you tell your factory how to run, without wiring a thousand switches.
    • CNC and Robotics take the human hands out of the danger zone.

    You have the power. Now you need the control.

    Win-path

    Trichlorosilane and Pure Silicon¶

    Unlocks after: Transistors, PLCs and Robots

    Before anyone gets to make diodes or transistors, build the silicon purification chain: MG-Si + HCl -> trichlorosilane -> 9N polysilicon -> Czochralski ingot -> raw wafers -> polished wafers.

    Do this: - retrieve React MG-Si with HCl gas to produce trichlorosilane (SiHCl3).
    yields: trichlorosilane×4 - retrieve Run the Siemens CVD bell-jar reactor to deposit 9N polysilicon on hot rods.
    yields: electronic-grade polysilicon×4 - retrieve Pull a Czochralski monocrystalline ingot and saw it into polished device-grade wafers.
    yields: Czochralski silicon ingot×1 polished silicon wafer×8

    Rewards: polished silicon wafer×4

    Win-path

    Diodes, Rectifiers and Transistors¶

    Unlocks after: Trichlorosilane and Pure Silicon

    After the wafer line is producing silicon_wafer_raw, set up a small electronics bench to make basic silicon diodes, rectifier bridges and low-power transistors for switching and amplification.

    Do this: - retrieve Have or place a glass electronics bench.
    yields: glass bench×1 - retrieve Process raw silicon wafers from your wafer line on the electronics bench.
    yields: raw silicon wafer×8 - retrieve Produce diodes, rectifier bridges and transistors.
    yields: small silicon diode×16 silicon bridge rectifier×4 signal NPN transistor×16

    Rewards: small silicon diode×8 signal NPN transistor×8

    Czochralski Wafers and Logic Chips¶

    Unlocks after: Trichlorosilane and Pure Silicon

    Fabricate integrated circuit logic chips from polished Czochralski wafers: define active regions by ion implantation, grow gate oxide, pattern and etch multiple metal interconnect layers by photolithography, then dice into individual chips.

    Do this: - retrieve Fab logic chips from polished wafers and copper interconnects.
    yields: logic chip×8

    Rewards: logic chip×4

    Carbon Fiber and CFRP¶

    Unlocks after: CNC Machines in the Shop

    Produce carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) structural panels: coal tar -> acrylonitrile (Sohio process) -> polyacrylonitrile (PAN) fiber -> stabilize -> carbonize at 1200°C -> impregnate with epoxy -> autoclave cure at 130-180°C.

    Do this: - retrieve Produce acrylonitrile via the Sohio process, then polymerize and spin PAN fiber.
    yields: acrylonitrile×4 PAN precursor fiber×2 - retrieve Stabilize PAN fiber and carbonize at 1200°C in nitrogen to form carbon fiber tow.
    yields: carbon fiber tow×4 - retrieve Produce epoxy resin and autoclave-cure CFRP structural panels.
    yields: epoxy resin×2 CFRP structural panel×2

    Rewards: CFRP structural panel×2

    Win-path

    Digital Monitoring and Alarms¶

    Unlocks after: Automate a Line with a PLC

    Add a digital overview panel and simple HMI screens that collect status from PLCs and sensors, so you can watch lines and acknowledge alarms from a central point. By now you should be reading the plant like an operator: station queue status, power/feed interruptions, pipe pressure/temperature, item transport issues, and output-space blocks all have visible diagnostic text.

    Do this: - retrieve Have or place HMI panels and a rugged industrial computer.
    yields: basic HMI panel×2 rugged industrial computer×1 - retrieve Install the AC grid mimic panel with metering instruments.
    yields: AC grid mimic panel×1 panel ammeter×4 panel voltmeter×4

    Rewards: panel ammeter×2 panel voltmeter×2
