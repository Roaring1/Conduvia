    Briefing

    Rockets, Reactors and Orbit¶

    Leaving the Ground¶

    You have an automated, solid-state industrial base. It produces electricity, refines materials, and cuts precision components while you sleep. So, what's next?

    We look up.

    Tier 6 is where your factory goes orbital. We aren't doing kerbal-style physics here; this is an engineering and logistics problem. You will build real launch capabilities: qualified engines, hyper-clean propellants, ground support equipment, telemetry, tracking, and the pad infrastructure to support it. If you build the system right, the rocket flies.

    • Test Stands and Propellants come first. Kerosene and Liquid Oxygen (LOX). You will run instrumented static fires before anything leaves the ground.
    • Orbital Launch Operations put the pad, stack, payload, and range safety together.
    • Satellite Networks and Lunar ISRU extend your reach.
    • Nuclear Base-Load Power ensures your planetary grid never stutters under the massive energy load of orbital operations.

    This is the ultimate test. None of this works if you didn't automate your Tier 3-5 supply chains. If you have to hand-craft steel plates to build a rocket engine, you've already failed. Let the factory do the work.

    Do this: - retrieve Fabricate and place the launch pad complex, loading umbilical, range-safety and tracking hardware.
    yields: basic launch pad complex×1 ground tracking radar×1 propellant loading umbilical×1 ground range-safety receiver×1 - retrieve Assemble the three-stage orbital rocket stack and tech-demo satellite.
    yields: orbital three-stage rocket stack×1 technology demonstrator satellite×1 - retrieve Run pad checkout, fuel the vehicle, and complete the orbital launch operation.
    yields: launch readiness review×1 fueled orbital launch vehicle×1 orbital insertion telemetry×1 deployed tech-demo satellite×1

    Titanium Processing Chain¶

    Unlocks after: Propellants and Test Stands

    Process ilmenite ore through the full titanium chain: fluidized-bed chlorination (TiO2 + 2Cl2 + 2C -> TiCl4 + 2CO) -> fractional distillation -> Hunter reduction (TiCl4 + 4Na -> Ti + 4NaCl) -> vacuum arc remelting (VAR, triple-melt for rotating parts) -> Ti-6Al-4V alloying -> plate forging and rolling.

    Do this: - retrieve Chlorinate ilmenite ore in a fluidized bed to produce TiCl4 ('tickle').
    yields: titanium tetrachloride×4 - retrieve Run Hunter reduction (TiCl4 + 4Na) and vacuum arc remelt the sponge.
    yields: titanium sponge×4 titanium ingot×2 - retrieve VAR-alloy Ti-6Al-4V and hot-roll titanium plate for structural use.
    yields: Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy×2 titanium alloy plate×4

    Rewards: titanium alloy plate×4

    Win-path

    Tungsten and Ferroniobium¶

    Unlocks after: Propellants and Test Stands

    Produce refractory and strategic metals: wolframite -> NaOH pressure digest -> solvent extraction -> APT precipitation -> calcine WO3 -> H2 reduction -> W powder -> press/sinter/swage W rod; and columbite -> HF digest -> MIBK solvent extraction -> Nb2O5 calcine -> aluminothermic reduction -> ferroniobium.

    Do this: - retrieve Digest wolframite in NaOH, solvent-extract, and calcine APT to produce WO3.
    yields: tungsten trioxide×4 - retrieve H2-reduce WO3 to W powder, cold-press, sinter at 2300u{00b0}C, and swage into dense W rods.
    yields: tungsten metal powder×4 sintered tungsten rod×2 - retrieve HF-digest columbite, MIBK-extract Nb, calcine to Nb2O5, and aluminothermically reduce to ferroniobium.
    yields: niobium pentoxide (Nb2O5)×2 ferroniobium×2

    Rewards: sintered tungsten rod×2 ferroniobium×2

    Win-path

    Zircaloy and Inconel Superalloy¶

    Unlocks after: Titanium Processing Chain, Tungsten and Ferroniobium

    Complete the high-performance alloy matrix. Zirconium chain: zircon -> carbo-chlorination -> ZrCl4 -> Hf separation (< 100 ppm Hf for nuclear grade) -> Kroll reduction -> vacuum-induction melt -> zircaloy-2 cladding alloy (Zr + 1.3% Sn + 0.1% Fe + 0.1% Cr). Superalloy chain: nickel cathode + ferrochrome + steel_ingot + ferroniobium -> VIM then VAR -> Inconel 718-like ingot.

    Do this: - retrieve Carbo-chlorinate zircon to ZrCl4 and Kroll-reduce to hafnium-free sponge.
    yields: zirconium tetrachloride×4 zirconium sponge×4 - retrieve Vacuum-induction melt zircaloy-2 from Hf-free sponge (nuclear-grade fuel rod cladding).
    yields: zircaloy cladding alloy×2 - retrieve VIM + VAR melt Inconel 718-like superalloy from nickel cathode + ferrochrome + ferroniobium.
    yields: Inconel superalloy ingot×2

    Rewards: zircaloy cladding alloy×2 Inconel superalloy ingot×1
