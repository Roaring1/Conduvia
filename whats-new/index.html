    What's New¶

    The latest industrial breakthroughs and expanded capabilities for your survival effort.

    Latest build — Refining, electronics & the road to orbit¶

    A massive expansion of your engineering footprint. The survival catalogue has grown by roughly 200 processes, and several critical industrial supply lines are now fully established.

    Quests are live in the guide¶

    The Climb pages now directly mirror your field logs—every tier breaks down into critical survival objectives and optional engineering branches. If a chemical reality forces a new approach, your blueprints update here.

    Petroleum & refining¶

    A full oil chain landed: pump crude with an oil pumpjack, clean it in a crude desalter, split it in the atmospheric refinery column, and finish fuels in the kerosene hydrotreater — backed by a refinery tank farm. This feeds fuels, plastics, and asphalt.

    Roads & construction¶

    Asphalt is now a real material line — binder, paving mix, and service pads — for building out durable ground infrastructure.

    Electronics fabrication¶

    The path to real circuitry is in: PCB fabrication line, electronics assembly bench, copper-clad laminate, ceramic IC packages, bond wire, and avionics PCB stacks — the groundwork for spacecraft avionics.

    The road to orbit¶

    Ground-side space infrastructure is now buildable: launch pad complex, ground station dish, rectenna field, hilltop relay station, high-gain spacecraft antenna, and supporting switchyard/cooling-tower builds.

    Logistics¶

    Move materials around your base with powered and wooden conveyor belts and wooden item pipes.

    Chain closures¶

    A large batch of "closure" recipes fills in gaps the audits had flagged — capture routes for wildlife, adobe/wall and concrete-from-slag paths, reagent stabilization, and more — so far fewer items are dead ends.

    Previously added¶

    • Heat & energy: fired stations are calibrated; recipe cards show real energy costs (kJ) and time.
    • Early crafting: camp infrastructure, pottery & vessels, rigging & haulage (windlass, capstan, block & tackle, potter's wheel, and more).
    • Power generation: Gas Engine Genset, Steam Turbine, Francis Water Turbine, Modern Wind Turbine, Solar PV Array.
    • Storage & electronics: Lead-Acid Battery Bank, Lithium-Ion & NiCd batteries, Soldering Iron, Cadmium, Silver Oxide.
